CareThread
A personal project, in progress

One thread, through every doctor you'll ever see.

Your health history shouldn't reset at every appointment. CareThread keeps your symptoms, medications, and visit notes in one place — so you can hand any doctor your story in seconds, not repeat it from memory.

Your records live in a dozen different portals. Your memory shouldn't have to be the thirteenth.

Every new doctor asks the same questions. What medications are you on. When did the symptoms start. Has this happened before. You end up reconstructing your own medical history from memory, in a waiting room, under time pressure.

CareThread is a running timeline you keep yourself — symptoms, prescriptions, appointments, and notes, logged as they happen. When you walk into a new appointment, you hand over a clear, plain-language summary instead of starting from zero.

It starts as a simple personal tool. The idea behind it is bigger — a layer of continuity that patients own, that could eventually connect across providers and catch the patterns no single doctor has time to see.

What changes for you

Your file travels with you. No more filling out the same forms every time you walk into a new hospital.

Never forget again

Past visits, medicines, and reports are all saved and ready. Nothing to remember, nothing to dig up.

Your health, all in one place

Sync your fitness tracker and see it right alongside your test results — the full picture, not just one piece.
