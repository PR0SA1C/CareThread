CareThread
A personal project, in progress

Your entire health history, connected.

Your medical records shouldn't be scattered across a dozen hospitals. CareThread uses your ABHA ID to instantly pull your prescriptions, scans, and blood reports into one secure, AI-powered timeline.

Built on the Ayushman Bharat Digital Mission.

CareThread is a unified ecosystem designed for both patients and healthcare providers in India. By integrating directly with the ABDM sandbox, we bypass manual KYC and link your records instantly using your ABHA ID.

For patients, it means true data ownership. For hospitals and doctors, it means access to a unified, AI-summarized patient history the moment you grant consent.

The result: A seamless flow of data between Radiology, Pathology, and the treating physician, without the patient ever having to carry a physical file again.

What changes for you

Works at every hospital

Your file travels with you. No more filling out the same forms every time you walk into a new hospital.

AI Health Trends

We analyze your past blood reports and scans using AI to draw long-term health trends automatically.

Wearable Integration

Sync your fitness trackers to compare daily biomarkers like heart rate directly against your clinical lab results.

Consent-driven Privacy

Through the ABDM network, you decide exactly which doctor or hospital gets access to your data, and for how long.
